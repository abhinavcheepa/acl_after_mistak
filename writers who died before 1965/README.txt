Place raw Hindi literary text files here.

Expected structure:
  <author_name>/
    <category>/
      *.txt  (UTF-8 encoded Hindi text)

Example:
  munshi premchand/
    kahani/
      godan.txt
      namak_ka_daroga.txt
  jaishankar prasad/
    natak/
      chandragupta.txt
